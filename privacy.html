Privacy

Betty Lou's Farm Hop (farmhop.fun) and the other games and sites made by Tuko. Last updated October 28, 2026.

Playing without an account

Your farm, sunflowers, hoppers, Fish Book and settings are saved only on your own device, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase (a Google service). We receive your Google account's name, email address and profile picture, and a copy of your game save is kept in your account so you can play on other devices.

Only you can read or change your save. We don't see your password, and we don't sell, share or use your information for advertising.

Playing with friends

When you play together, the game passes your moves and your hopper choice between your devices, directly or through a relay server. The relay doesn't store or log what's sent.

Hop Supporters

Payments are handled by Stripe or Ko-fi; we never see your card details. To unlock the supporter perks we keep your supporter code, the amount, your name as given at checkout, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your email so you can restore the perks on another device.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings > Account > Delete my cloud saves and account. This removes your saves and your sign-in from our records. Your save on each device stays until you clear it there.
